Design Inspiration

Two reference designs that define the visual DNA of this product.

What We Take From Each

From Coffee Craft (Design 02)

  • Layout structure — arched hero cards, bold logo lockup, clear screen hierarchy
  • Navigation — bottom tab bar with icon-only nav, clean and unobtrusive
  • Cart screen — list-based with item thumbnails, quantity controls, coupon field
  • Typography — large, confident headings; tight body text
  • Overall mood — premium, confident, brand-forward

From Warm Beige (Design 04)

  • Color palette — warm off-white base (#F5EDE3), terracotta/clay CTA buttons
  • Photography style — soft-lit food on neutral backgrounds, objects floating on cream
  • Card style — minimal frames, lots of breathing room, soft shadows
  • Form fields — rounded pill inputs with light strokes
  • Overall mood — warm, approachable, artisanal

Merged Design System

Philosophy

Structured warmth. The confidence of a premium brand, the approachability of a neighborhood restaurant.

Think: Coffee Craft's bones, dressed in the Warm Beige's palette.

Rationale: Playfair Display gives the brand personality and warmth (from Design 04's editorial feel), while Inter handles all functional UI (clean and legible like Coffee Craft).

Component Patterns

Buttons

  • Primary: Fully rounded pill (border-radius: 999px), terracotta fill, white text, 52px height
  • Secondary: Pill outline, accent border, transparent fill
  • Ghost: No border, muted text, used for secondary actions
  • Icon Button: Circle, 44px, used for cart, back, search

Cards (Food Items)

  • border-radius: 20px, warm white background, shadow-sm
  • Top: full-width food photo (16:9), object-cover, floating/soft-lit style
  • Bottom: name (Inter 600), short description (muted), price (accent color) + add button
  • On tap: scale to 0.97 then back (spring animation, 200ms)

Bottom Navigation (from Coffee Craft)

  • 4–5 icon-only tabs
  • Active tab: accent color icon + small dot indicator
  • Inactive: muted gray
  • Background: bg-surface with top border, no shadow

Category Tabs (horizontal scroll)

  • Pill-shaped, 36px height
  • Active: brand-dark fill, white label
  • Inactive: bg-surface, text-muted label
  • Smooth slide indicator on switch

Hero Section (from Coffee Craft)

  • Arched or large rounded card pinned to top of menu
  • Restaurant logo centered, cover photo as background with dark overlay
  • Restaurant name in Playfair Display, large

Form Inputs (from Warm Beige)

  • Rounded pill inputs, 52px height
  • Warm border (#E8DDD5), no harsh outlines
  • Focus state: accent border color
  • Labels above field, Inter 13px medium

Order Status Stepper

  • Horizontal steps: Received → Preparing → Ready → Served
  • Completed steps: brand-dark fill
  • Active step: accent with pulse animation
  • Pending: muted border

Dark Mode Approach

Dark mode uses warm darks, not cold grays. The background is #1A1A14 — a near-black with a slight warm green tint (borrowing from Coffee Craft's forest green DNA). Cards lift to #2A2920. The terracotta accent holds across both modes, making it a consistent brand signal.

This avoids the typical "dark mode feels like a different app" problem.

What NOT to Do

  • ❌ Cold grays anywhere — always use warm-tinted neutrals
  • ❌ Blue links or blue focus rings — use clay or forest instead
  • ❌ Sharp corners — minimum rounded-xl on all interactive elements
  • ❌ Heavy drop shadows — keep shadows soft and warm-tinted
  • ❌ White backgrounds on dark mode — use dark-surface tokens
  • ❌ San-serif for restaurant/hero headings — Playfair Display is the brand voice
